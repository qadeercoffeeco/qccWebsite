Privacy Policy

How Qadeer Coffee Co handles inquiry information.

This policy explains how we collect and use information submitted through our website, booking form, analytics tools, and advertising platforms.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Qadeer Coffee Co collects information you choose to submit, including your name, email address, phone number, event date, event location, guest count, event type, and notes about your event.

We use this information to respond to inquiries, provide quotes, plan event service, coordinate bookings, improve our website, and measure the performance of ads and marketing campaigns.

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand page visits, referral sources, and interactions such as availability button clicks. Google Analytics may collect online identifiers and browsing information through cookies or similar technologies. We may also use advertising tools, including Meta technologies, to measure conversions and improve future campaigns.

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website, manage forms, communicate with clients, process bookings, or measure marketing performance.

You can contact us through the booking form on our website to request an update or deletion of inquiry information you previously submitted.
