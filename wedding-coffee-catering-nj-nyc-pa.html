Yes. Qadeer Coffee Co can help build signature lattes, matcha drinks, and custom mocktails around your favorite flavors, colors, names, story, or wedding aesthetic.

Yes. The cart can support bridal showers, welcome events, wedding mornings, receptions, walimas, mehndis, after parties, and private wedding weekend gatherings.

Do you offer alcohol free wedding beverage options?

Yes. We specialize in coffee, matcha, and mocktails for couples who want memorable drinks without alcohol.
