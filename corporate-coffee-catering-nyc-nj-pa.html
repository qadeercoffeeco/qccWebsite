Corporate FAQ

Questions corporate teams ask before booking.

Can Qadeer Coffee Co provide a COI?

Yes. Qadeer Coffee Co can provide Certificate of Insurance documentation for corporate bookings and venue requirements when requested during the booking process.

Is the cart a fit for brand activations?

Yes. We can create custom drinks, menus, branded cups, stickers, and printed latte art for product launches, retail events, popups, and client activations.

Can you support conferences and large guest counts?

Yes. We can plan service for conferences, conventions, registration windows, networking breaks, and other busy moments based on the guest count and event schedule.

What regions do you serve for corporate events?

Qadeer Coffee Co serves corporate events across New York City, New Jersey, and Pennsylvania, with select regional events considered based on size, timing, and availability.
