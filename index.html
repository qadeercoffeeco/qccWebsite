Our story

A family cart, built around care.

Sarmed Qadeer founded Qadeer Coffee Co with his sisters, Raadiya and Naaima, to bring memorable coffee and matcha experiences to every gathering.

Today, their sibling-founded cart serves weddings, corporate events, and private celebrations across Pennsylvania, New Jersey, New York City, and the DMV.
