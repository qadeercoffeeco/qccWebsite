Conference FAQ

Questions event teams ask before booking.

Can the coffee cart be used to drive booth traffic?

Yes. A fresh coffee, matcha, or mocktail gives attendees a practical reason to stop and gives your team a natural way to start a conversation.

Can Qadeer Coffee Co handle large guest counts?

Yes. We plan the menu, staffing, equipment, and service flow around your guest count, schedule, and expected rush times.

Can the menu be branded for a sponsor or exhibitor?

Yes. Menus can include branded drink names, custom stickers, campaign flavors, and a beverage lineup that supports the sponsor, exhibitor, or event theme.

What drinks work best for conferences?

Espresso drinks, iced lattes, matcha lattes, handcrafted mocktails, and bottled lattes can all work well. The best menu depends on service speed, event timing, and attendee volume.
